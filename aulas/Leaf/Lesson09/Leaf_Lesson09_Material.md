---

> 💡 **Word Tip — "Eventually" vs "Finally":**
> - **Eventually** = depois de um período / no fim das contas → mais aberto no tempo
> - **Finally** = depois de esperar / tentar bastante → conclusão esperada
> - *Eventually, she found a new job.* (depois de algum tempo)
> - *Finally, the bus arrived!* (depois de muito esperar)

---

> 💡 **Word Tip — "Twist" vs "Ending":**
> - **Twist** = uma mudança inesperada no meio ou no fim da história
> - **Ending** = simplesmente como a história termina
> - *The twist was that the detective was the criminal.*
> - *The ending was happy, but surprising.*

## 📚 2. Grammar Focus

### 2.1 The Three Narrative Tenses — Overview

Ao contar histórias em inglês, três tempos do passado funcionam juntos:

| Tense | Função | Palavras-chave |
|---|---|---|
| **Past Simple** | Eventos principais — o que aconteceu | then, suddenly, after that, finally |
| **Past Continuous** | Fundo / ação em progresso | while, when, at that moment, at the time |
| **Past Perfect** | Evento anterior ao momento narrado | before, already, by the time, after, never |

**Exemplo combinado:**

> *She **was walking** home when she **heard** a strange sound.*
> *She **had never heard** anything like it before.*

- **was walking** → ação de fundo (Past Continuous)
- **heard** → evento principal (Past Simple)
- **had never heard** → antes daquele momento (Past Perfect)

---

### 2.2 Past Simple — The Main Events

O **Past Simple** é o tempo principal das narrativas. Ele move a história para frente.

**Estrutura:** Sujeito + verbo no passado

| Verbo Regular | Verbo Irregular |
|---|---|
| *She **walked** into the room.* | *He **saw** something unusual.* |
| *They **called** for help.* | *She **ran** as fast as she could.* |
| *I **decided** to stay.* | *We **left** before midnight.* |

**Sequência de eventos:**

> *First, she **opened** the door. Then, she **walked** inside. After that, she **noticed** something was wrong. Finally, she **called** the police.*

> 💡 Use o **Past Simple** sempre que o próximo evento da sequência acontece.

---

### 2.3 Past Continuous — The Background

O **Past Continuous** descreve o que **já estava acontecendo** quando algo novo ocorreu.

**Estrutura:** Sujeito + was / were + verbo-ing

**Criando o cenário:**

| Contexto | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| tempo / clima | *It **was raining** outside.* |
| ações de personagens | *Everyone **was talking** at the same time.* |
| atmosfera | *The children **were playing** in the street.* |

**Padrão de interrupção:**

| Estrutura | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| **While** + Past Continuous + Past Simple | *While she **was reading**, the phone **rang**.* |
| Past Simple + **when** + Past Continuous | *He **arrived** when they **were still waiting**.* |

> 💡 O Past Continuous descreve o que **já estava em progresso**.  
> O Past Simple descreve o que **interrompeu ou aconteceu em seguida**.

---

### 2.4 Past Simple vs Past Continuous — Comparação

| Situação | Tempo Verbal | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Ação completa em momento específico | Past Simple | *She **left** at 10 o'clock.* |
| Ação em progresso em momento específico | Past Continuous | *At 10, she **was leaving** the building.* |
| Sequência de eventos | Past Simple | *He **opened** the window and **looked** outside.* |
| Descrição de fundo | Past Continuous | *The sun **was setting** and the birds **were singing**.* |
| Ação interrompida | Past Cont. + Past Simple | *I **was cooking** when the alarm **went off**.* |

---

### 2.5 Past Perfect — Before the Story

O **Past Perfect** descreve uma ação que aconteceu **antes** de outro momento no passado.

**Estrutura:** Sujeito + had + particípio passado

| Uso | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| Ação antes de outro evento passado | *She **had left** before he **arrived**.* |
| Explicar a causa de uma situação | *He **was** tired because he **hadn't slept** well.* |
| Informação de fundo | *They **had never met** before that night.* |

**Lógica de sequência:**

> *By the time the police arrived, the thief **had already escaped**.*

- Primeiro: o ladrão escapou → **Past Perfect**
- Depois: a polícia chegou → **Past Simple**

**Palavras-chave do Past Perfect:**

| Palavra / Expressão | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| **already** | *She had already eaten when he called.* |
| **by the time** | *By the time we arrived, the movie had started.* |
| **before** | *He had spoken to her before the meeting.* |
| **after** | *After they had finished, they went home.* |
| **never / ever** | *I had never seen anything like it.* |

> 💡 O Past Perfect não é sempre obrigatório. Use-o quando a **ordem dos eventos** precisa ser clara.

---

### 2.6 Time Sequencers — Organizando a Narrativa

**Início da história:**

| Expressão | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| **Once** | *Once, a young traveler arrived at a strange village.* |
| **One day** | *One day, she found an old letter in the attic.* |
| **It all started when...** | *It all started when he missed his train.* |
| **At first** | *At first, everything seemed normal.* |

**Durante a história:**

| Expressão | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| **Then** | *Then, she noticed the window was open.* |
| **After that** | *After that, things moved quickly.* |
| **Meanwhile** | *Meanwhile, her friends were waiting outside.* |
| **Suddenly** | *Suddenly, the lights went out.* |
| **At that moment** | *At that moment, she realized the truth.* |
| **A few minutes later** | *A few minutes later, the police arrived.* |

**Final da história:**

| Expressão | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| **Eventually** | *Eventually, they found a solution.* |
| **In the end** | *In the end, she decided to stay.* |
| **Finally** | *Finally, he opened the letter.* |
| **After all that** | *After all that, they went home exhausted.* |

---

### 2.7 Story Linking Words — Conectando Ideias

**Causa e efeito:**

| Palavra | Uso | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **because** | explica a razão | *She was scared because the door was locked.* |
| **so** | mostra resultado | *It was raining, so they stayed inside.* |
| **as a result** | resultado formal | *The storm arrived early. As a result, the event was cancelled.* |
| **that's why** | explica consequência | *He had no money. That's why he didn't go.* |

**Contraste e surpresa:**

| Palavra | Uso | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **but** | contraste simples | *She wanted to leave, but she couldn't.* |
| **however** | contraste formal | *It seemed safe. However, there was a hidden danger.* |
| **although** | contraste dentro de cláusula | *Although she was tired, she kept walking.* |
| **unexpectedly** | virada surpresa | *Unexpectedly, he appeared at the door.* |

---

### 2.8 Story Structure — Estrutura da Narrativa

Uma boa história tem três partes:

**Parte 1 — Criando o cenário (Início)**
> Apresente os **personagens**, o **lugar** e a **situação**.  
> Use Past Continuous para o fundo + Past Simple para o primeiro evento.

*It was a cold winter evening. The streets **were empty** and the wind **was blowing** hard. A woman named Clara **was walking** home alone when she suddenly **heard** footsteps behind her.*

---

**Parte 2 — O problema / a ação (Meio)**
> Desenvolva o **conflito** ou ação principal.  
> Use Past Simple para sequência + sequenciadores de tempo.

*She **turned** around, but no one **was there**. Then, she **noticed** a shadow near the wall. Suddenly, a cat **jumped** out of a bin and **ran** across the street. Clara **laughed** at herself and **continued** walking.*

---

**Parte 3 — A resolução (Final)**
> Mostre como a história **se resolve**.  
> Use eventually, in the end, finally + Past Simple.

*Eventually, she **arrived** home safe and warm. By the time she **made** a cup of tea, she **had already forgotten** about the scare. In the end, it **was** just a quiet evening — nothing more.*

## 📘 3. Phrasal Verbs for Storytelling

Estes phrasal verbs aparecem com frequência em narrativas, histórias e relatos pessoais.

| Phrasal Verb | Tradução | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **end up** | acabar em / terminar de determinada forma | *They ended up sleeping on the floor.* |
| **turn out** | resultar em / descobrir que | *It turned out that no one had left.* |
| **come across** | encontrar por acaso / causar impressão | *She came across an old photo in the drawer.* |
| **go through** | passar por (algo difícil ou intenso) | *He went through a very difficult year.* |
| **make up** | inventar (história ou mentira) | *She made up an excuse and left early.* |
| **carry on** | continuar (apesar de algo) | *Despite the rain, they carried on walking.* |
| **break down** | desmoronar / perder o controle emocional | *He broke down when he heard the news.* |
| **grow up** | crescer / ter determinada criação | *She grew up in a small village in the mountains.* |
| **set out** | partir com objetivo / começar jornada | *They set out early in the morning.* |
| **fall apart** | desmoronar / se desfazer | *Without a leader, the team began to fall apart.* |
| **bring up** | mencionar / criar (um filho) | *No one brought up the argument from the night before.* |
| **pass away** | falecer (forma mais suave que "die") | *His grandfather had passed away the year before.* |

---

> 💡 **Phrasal Verb Tip — "End up" + -ING:**  
> Após **end up**, use sempre o verbo na forma **-ing**:  
> ✅ *They ended up **staying** the whole weekend.*  
> ❌ *They ended up **to stay** the whole weekend.*

---

> 💡 **Phrasal Verb Tip — "Come across" com dois significados:**
> - **Encontrar por acaso:** *I came across this photo while cleaning.*
> - **Causar uma impressão:** *She came across as confident and calm.*  
>   (= Ela causou uma impressão de confiança e calma)

---

> 💡 **Phrasal Verb Tip — "Turn out" vs "End up":**
> - **Turn out** = focado no **resultado ou descoberta**  
>   *It turned out that he was right all along.*
> - **End up** = focado na **situação final inesperada**  
>   *We ended up in a completely different city.*

---

### 3.1 Phrasal Verbs — Contextos Narrativos

**End up:**

| Contexto | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| Resultado inesperado | *We took the wrong road and ended up in the next town.* |
| Estado final | *After hours of arguing, they ended up agreeing.* |
| Situação não planejada | *He ended up staying for three weeks.* |

**Turn out:**

| Contexto | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| Resultado diferente do esperado | *It turned out to be a great night.* |
| Descoberta de algo verdadeiro | *It turned out that she had been there the whole time.* |
| Com "well" / "badly" | *Fortunately, everything turned out well.* |

**Come across:**

| Contexto | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| Encontrar por acaso | *I came across his name in an old newspaper.* |
| Causar impressão | *The villain came across as charming at first.* |

---

### 3.2 Formal vs Everyday Narrative Language

| Everyday (Falado) | Mais Formal / Literário | Tradução |
|---|---|---|
| *She ended up in trouble.* | *She found herself in a difficult situation.* | acabou em apuros |
| *He made up a story.* | *He fabricated an account.* | inventou uma história |
| *Things fell apart.* | *The situation deteriorated rapidly.* | as coisas desmoronaram |
| *She came across a letter.* | *She discovered / encountered a letter.* | encontrou uma carta |
| *He went through a lot.* | *He endured a great deal of hardship.* | passou por muito |
| *They carried on.* | *They persevered / continued their journey.* | continuaram |

---

### 3.3 Common Mistakes — Erros Comuns

| ❌ Incorrect | ✅ Correct | Explicação |
|---|---|---|
| *While she drove, the car stopped.* | *While she **was driving**, the car stopped.* | Ações de fundo usam Past Continuous |
| *When I arrived, she cooked.* | *When I arrived, she **was cooking**.* | Ela já estava cozinhando — use Past Continuous |
| *She left before he arrived.* (quando a ordem importa) | *She **had left** before he arrived.* | Past Perfect mostra qual evento veio primeiro |
| *I ended up to stay at home.* | *I ended up **staying** at home.* | "End up" é seguido de **-ing** |
| *He come across a solution.* | *He **came across** a solution.* | Use a forma passada do verbo principal |
| *Suddenly he is gone.* | *Suddenly he **was gone**.* | Use passado em narrativas |
| *They setted out at dawn.* | *They **set out** at dawn.* | "Set" é irregular — passado é **set** |
| *She growed up in Rio.* | *She **grew up** in Rio.* | "Grow" é irregular — passado é **grew** |

## 📖 4. Full Story Example

**Context:** Uma mulher conta uma história sobre uma noite inesperada.

---

> *One evening, Clara **was sitting** alone in her flat. The rain **was falling** quietly outside.*
>
> *She **had been** home all day and **had finished** her work early. Suddenly, her phone **rang**. It **was** an old friend she **hadn't spoken** to in years.*
>
> *"Come to the café," her friend **said**. "I need to talk to you."*
>
> *Clara **hesitated**, but **decided** to go. She **put on** her coat and **set out** into the rain.*
>
> *When she **arrived**, her friend **was sitting** at a corner table. He **looked** nervous. He **brought up** something she **had never expected** to hear — he **had found** the letter she **had written** ten years before but **had never sent**.*
>
> *She **went through** a rush of emotions. She **didn't know** what to say.*
>
> *Eventually, they **talked** for hours. In the end, it **turned out** to be one of the best conversations she **had ever had**.*
>
> *She **ended up** walking home in the rain — but this time, she **was smiling**.*

---

**Elementos narrativos usados:**
- ✅ **Past Continuous** para o cenário: *was sitting, was falling*
- ✅ **Past Perfect** para o fundo: *had been, hadn't spoken, had never sent*
- ✅ **Past Simple** para eventos principais: *rang, decided, arrived, talked*
- ✅ **Sequenciadores de tempo**: *suddenly, when, eventually, in the end*
- ✅ **Phrasal verbs**: *put on, set out, brought up, turned out, ended up, went through*
- ✅ **Palavras de ligação**: *but, so*
